      What I can delete on request

      Nothing. This app sends no data to me or to any service working for me, so there is no server-side copy of anything to delete.

      How to ask

      Because there is no account, there is no name or email to look you up by. Fill this in and the text it produces is everything I need to find and delete what is yours. Nothing you type here leaves your browser — this page has no server. Copy the text and send it to the developer contact details published with the app on its Google Play listing. Google requires those to be current, so they are the address that reaches me.

      What I cannot delete
      
    
      Google is the merchant for anything bought through Google Play, and Google keeps its own record
    of the transaction for tax and accounting reasons. That record is Google's, not mine, and is
    governed by Google's privacy policy — deleting
    my copy does not remove theirs, and I have no way to make it.
